Get ready to witness the next generation of innovators, creators, and problem-solvers!

STARATHON 2027 is an inter-college hackathon organized by the Department of Information Technology at Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan College of Engineering and Technology, Chennai.

Bringing together talented students from different colleges, STARATHON is a platform to explore ideas, showcase skills, collaborate with brilliant minds, and turn creativity into innovation.

Whether you are a tech enthusiast, a creative thinker, or someone who loves taking on new challenges, this is your stage to shine.
